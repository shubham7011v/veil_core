**How to Play Bluff:**
1. Players take turns playing cards face down in the center pile
2. Declare what rank of cards you're playing (e.g., "Two Kings")
3. You can play the actual cards or **BLUFF** with different cards
4. Any player can call "BLUFF!" if they suspect you're lying
5. If caught bluffing, you pick up the entire pile. If you were honest, the accuser picks up the pile!
6. First player to get rid of all their cards wins the game!
